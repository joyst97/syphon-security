COMPREHENSIVE FEATURE SUITE

Explore the full power of JOYST CORPORATION Discord Security & Automation Platform.

1. Anti-Nuke & Quarantine Shield

Protects your Discord server against rouge administrators, compromised bot tokens, and malicious account hacks. When an unauthorized user attempts to mass delete channels, wipe roles, or generate mass webhooks, JOYST CORPORATION revokes their administrative privileges in < 0.3 seconds and logs the exact violation event in #joyst-security-logs.

2. AutoMod & Incident Logging

Monitors chat messages in real time for toxic slurs, phishing links, invite spam, and character flood abuse. When a violation is flagged, the message content, offending user ID, channel link, and attachment URLs are rendered directly inside markdown codeblocks in your security logs channel.

3. Zero-Disk Direct Audio Stream Engine

Streams high-definition YouTube audio directly into Voice Channels without downloading local files. 0% disk storage usage on your host server. Features interactive play, pause, skip, loop track/queue, autoplay recommendations, and requester-protected buttons.

4. User Info & Alt Risk Inspector

Inspect account creation timestamps, server tenure, key admin permissions, roles, and Alt Risk Scores (🔴 High Risk < 7 days, 🟡 Medium Risk 7-30 days, 🟢 Low Risk > 30 days) via /userinfo command.

5. Giveaways & Support Ticket Panels

Automate community giveaways with custom button claims and winner rerolls. Manage member support inquiries through interactive ticket dropdown menus with automatic transcript generation.
